# Vehicle rental GEO playbook

## Example intent clusters (research, do not assume current facts)
- Monthly car rental Phuket / аренда авто на Пхукете на месяц
- Car rental Patong / аренда автомобиля Патонг
- Phuket car rental deposit / без депозита
- Phuket airport car delivery / доставка автомобиля в аэропорт
- Car rental insurance excess Thailand / страховка и франшиза
- Economy vs SUV rental Phuket / выбор класса автомобиля
- Documents and driving licence requirements for visitors to Thailand
- Rental cancellation, pickup, fuel policy, mileage and late returns

## Page recommendations
- Create a trustworthy overview of fleet categories and up-to-date terms, not fictional inventory.
- Give each available vehicle a unique factual page with model, year, images, features, verified rate conditions and booking CTA.
- Publish transparent guides to deposit, insurance, delivery, driving requirements, monthly pricing and return process, using approved policy text.
- Add localized English, Russian and Thai content only if business supports each language. Verify hreflang and consistent terms.
- Connect FAQs to the corresponding transactional page and the booking flow.

## Conversion safeguards
- Do not claim zero deposit for every booking if conditions vary.
- Do not claim delivery is free if it depends on area, dates or promotional terms.
- Do not imply full insurance without exclusions or excess.
- Never expose customer PII or internal CRM identifiers in public pages.

## Suggested deliverables
- Top 20 questions with mapped URLs and search intent
- Technical audit matrix
- Prioritized content brief for 5 highest-value pages
- JSON-LD changes with validation notes
- 30/60/90-day rollout and measurement plan
